## Act 2: Mandatory Bee Wearable AI Flow (0:45 - 2:00)
- **Speaker:** "Let's demonstrate our primary track: Bee Wearable AI."
- **Visual:**
  1. Click **Evermind Memory** on the sidebar.
  2. Show the **Pending Review** queue and explain the Zero-Fabrication rule:
     - *"Everlife AI never invents memories. Recordings captured by Bee are ingested and extracted into candidate memories, requiring explicit user approval before permanent persistence."*
  3. Click **Import Wearable Context** (or demonstrate live `bee now` if authenticated).
  4. Paste or show incoming utterance:
     - *"We decided to submit Everlife AI for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026 under the Bee Wearable AI track. I will deliver the complete architecture diagram and README by Friday 5 PM. The budget is allocated at 500 dollars for AWS credits."*
  5. Click **Ingest & Extract**.
  6. Point out the extracted cards:
     - Shows type (`commitment`, `fact`, `decision`).
     - Shows exact evidence quote from the transcript.
     - Shows confidence score.
  7. Click **Approve & Persist** on the commitment item.
  8. Switch to **Approved Memories** to show it permanently stored in SQLite and ready for RAG query.
